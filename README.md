# Telecom Tax & Surcharge Audit

Audit telecom taxes and regulatory surcharges against service type, jurisdiction, exemption, and contribution rules.

**Primary buyer:** Enterprises, carriers, and communications platforms. **Evidence:** telecom invoices, service addresses, product codes, jurisdiction mapping, federal and state taxes, USF, regulatory fees, exemptions, credits, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Carrier invoice ingestion
- Service product classification
- Service address validation
- Jurisdiction mapping
- Federal USF calculation
- State USF calculation
- 911 surcharge validation
- Gross receipts tax review
- Utility user tax review
- Internet access exemption
- Resale exemption
- Overcharge calculation
- Carrier refund request
- Tax authority claim workflow
- Service jurisdiction analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4688>. API: `5688`.
